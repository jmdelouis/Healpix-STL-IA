# Changelog

Every change of the code or of its behaviour is recorded here, newest first, together with the documentation pages it updates (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## Unreleased

### Fixed
- **Run directory.** The scripts write the runs and the Slurm logs to `$RUNS` (default `$R/runs`), so that they can go to another file system when the work one is full. Documentation updated: `REPRODUCE.md` (environment, pitfalls).
- **Checkpoints.** `stlia-train-denoise` writes `model.pt` to a temporary file and renames it. A failed write, such as a full file system, now leaves the previous checkpoint intact instead of a truncated file. No documentation change: the outputs are the same.

### Changed
- **No assistant name in the scripts, code comments and docs.** Branch names of STL-Dev-MPI replaced by their commits (scripts/*.slurm, reproduce.sh, REPRODUCE.md, docs, stlia/spectra.py, stlia/train_denoise.py). No change of behaviour.
- **Paper: why the native network removes less rms than PyTorch.** Sect. 6 explains the 0.70 against 0.80. With P(x) = x - QU(EB(x)), the part lost in the round trip through the E and B maps, P holds 0.47 of the noise rms. The PyTorch residual d - u is 0.68 inside the E/B maps and 0.32 in P. The native one is 0.67 and 0.01, since it returns u = d - QU(d_EB - u_EB) and passes P(d) through unchanged. Same removal within E/B. No change of code.
- **Paper: the narrow native network on the E and B maps.** Sect. 6 adds `n256_eb_c16` (table row, paragraph): 16-64 channels per level, the same removed rms as `n256_eb` (0.70, 0.71), d - u at 0.90 of the FFP10 noise for 10 <= l < 30 against 1.54, and spectra of u within 6 % of the cross spectrum up to l = 300 at |b| > 60 deg; 2.7 s per step. The best setting so far. No change of code.
- **Paper: the joint native network on the E and B maps.** Sect. 6 adds `n256_eb` (table row, BICEP2 field, full-sky maps and spectra, paragraph). The EE spectrum of d - u is 2.0 times the half-mission noise for 10 <= l < 30 against 10.6 with the spin-2 front end, which confirms the l^4 weight as the cause; spectra of u within 2 % of the cross spectrum up to l = 300 over |b| > 30 deg, removed rms 0.70, 2.6 s per step on an A100. No change of code.
- **Paper: cost of the bias estimate.** The bias-estimate row of the native-network timing table is removed: its A100 figures (16.1 s native, 9.8 s PyTorch) were not measured like for like. Re-timed at nside 128 on a T4 with the same settings (one field, 40 draws, four masks), one estimate takes 10.8 s native against 12.1 s through the stlia engine, after a first native call of 17.5 s that builds the GPU engines; a statistic costs the same in both paths. The cost paragraph now gives these numbers. No change of code.
- **Paper: the joint native network with a spin-2 front end.** Sect. 6 adds `n256_spin` (table row, paragraph): 3.0 s per step on an A100 for the pair, spectra of u within 1 % of the cross spectrum up to l = 300, but d - u is 8 times the noise for 10 <= l < 30, likely because the statistics on conj-eth^2 (Q + iU) weight the multipoles as l^4. The paragraph also gives the nside 128 -> 256 coarse-to-fine run as a worse case. The paper no longer names the development branch of `NativeUNet`. No change of code.
- **Paper: the narrow E/B network over 3000 steps.** Sect. 5 adds `r3k_eb_c16` (table row, BICEP2 field, full-sky maps and spectra, paragraph). It removes 0.80 and 0.77 of the noise rms against 0.69 and 0.73 after 1000 steps, with spectra within 1 % of the cross spectrum up to l = 300 over |b| > 30 deg. `scripts/reproduce.sh` adds `n256_spin` and `n256_eb`, the joint native network of STL-Dev-MPI (main 45d3e17, built in `$R/stl_nj`). No change of code.
- **Paper: gauge and spin-2 layers.** Sect. 2 cites McEwen (2026) on why a directional stencil with output on the sphere needs a gauge; the discussion proposes an input layer (Q, U) -> (E, B) and its inverse at the output, which remove the harmonic transforms from the training loop. New page `docs/rust_port.md`: profile of a step (the statistics are about 90 %) and the plan to train on E and B entirely in the native engine. No change of code.
- **Paper: training on discs, and the statistics of the removed part.** Sect. 5 adds `pt64_cells` (disc training on the 48 cells of nside 2) against its full-sky control `t64_jebl3p`, as a negative result: a loss on discs does not constrain the large scales. It also adds `r1k_res` against `r1k_ref`: Phi(d - u) against Phi(n) in place of `l2` removes more rms but breaks the data term and the high-latitude BB spectrum. The discussion no longer proposes disc patches beyond nside 512. Both nside 64 runs added to `scripts/reproduce.sh` (`gpup`, the GPU disc wrapper). No change of code.
- **Paper: seed and width of the network.** Sect. 5 adds the second seed of `r1k_eb` (`r1k_eb_s1`, text) and the network with 16-64 channels per level (`r1k_eb_c16`: table row, BICEP2 field, full-sky maps and spectra, paragraph). No change of code.
- **Paper: per-map reference on the E and B maps.** Sect. 5 adds `l1k_eb` (table row, BICEP2 field, full-sky maps and spectra, paragraph). No change of code.
- **Paper: statistics on the E and B maps.** Sect. 5 adds `r1k_eb` (table row, BICEP2 field, full-sky maps and spectra) against `r1k_ref`, and `r1k_ebx` as a negative result: at nside 256, `xcl` removes the large scales. Fixed a broken `\rm` and `\ref` in the paragraph on `r256_joint_eebb`. Run `r1k_eb_s1` (second seed) added to `scripts/reproduce.sh`. No change of code.
- **Paper brought up to date.** Sect. 5 adds `l256_fz_cl`, and the high-latitude ratios over |b| > 60 deg. Removed: `l256_fz`, the L-BFGS run with a frozen bias estimated for every coefficient, which the current scheme no longer uses. Its line is also removed from `scripts/reproduce.sh` and `REPRODUCE.md`; `--bias-all` stays. Rewritten: the abstract, the introduction and the discussion, which described the first test at nside 64; the paragraphs saying runs were in progress; the per-map reference, which now freezes biases when the network run does; and the code paragraph of the appendix, which now points to `REPRODUCE.md` for the engine commit of each run. Documentation updated: method (`--bias-all`), `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **High-latitude spectrum mask.** `l256_fz_cl`, `r256_joint_eebb` and `r256_fz_cl857` in `scripts/reproduce.sh` use `--cl-masks 0,30,45,60`, and the STL-Dev-MPI build of `$R/stl_sp2`: with masks up to 45 degrees only, the spectra of u at |b| > 60 deg were the least constrained. No change of code.
- **Frozen biases only where there is a noise bias.** With `--bias frozen`, the bias is zero for the coefficients whose noise average is the noise-free statistic: the noise enters them only linearly. These are the mean of `data`, `S2` of `l1`, `l2` and `l3`, and the `S3` of `l2`/`l3` with the noise-free map under the modulus (`unbiased()`). `--bias-all` restores the previous estimate for every coefficient, as used by `l256_fz`, whose line in `scripts/reproduce.sh` now has it. Documentation updated: method (*Which coefficients have a noise bias*), paper Sect. 2.4 (Table of biased coefficients).
- **No noise bias on the spectrum loss.** The model of `cl` is now `C_b(u, u)`, with live and frozen biases alike. The half-mission noises are independent, so `<C_b(u + n1, u + n2)> = C_b(u, u)`. Before, the live model averaged noise draws, which only added scatter to the gradient. The frozen model subtracted an estimated bias, close to zero but noisy, which could make the solution drift. Documentation updated: method (*Spectrum loss*), paper Sect. 2.4.

### Added
- **Disc training on the PR3 maps.** `stlia-train-patches --real` trains on discs with the terms of the full-sky runs (`data`, `l1`, `l2`, `l3`, `cl`), with the options `--joint`, `--pol-stats {qu,eb}`, `--cond`, `--bias {live,frozen}`, `--bias-all` and `--cl-*` (`stlia.patch_real`). E and B are computed on the tapered disc. `--centre-nside 2` trains on the 48 cells of nside 2. `UNet.forward_patches` accepts the joint network (`maps=2`). `stlia-train-denoise --norm pixel` gives a full-sky control with the same network. At nside 64, the cells give unbiased spectra but remove almost no noise below ℓ = 60. Test added. Documentation updated: method (*Training on disc patches*).
- **Per-map reference of the joint network.** `--joint --optimizer lbfgs` minimises the joint terms, on (Q, U) or on the E and B maps (`--pol-stats eb`), over the pixels of the pair (`run_lbfgs_joint`). Documentation updated: method (*A joint (Q, U) network*).
- **Decorrelation term `xcl`, and noise at the input.** `xcl` asks the cross spectrum of `u` and `d - u` to vanish (`stlia.spectra.DecorrelationLoss`), with a variance fixed by the half-mission maps. `--input-noise ALPHA` trains the network on `d + ALPHA n`, `n` a training noise simulation. At nside 64, `rcl` caused the excess of power of `u` at |b| > 60 deg and the removal of large scales, and `tcl` changed nothing; `xcl` with a weight of 1 removes more noise without changing the spectra of `u`. Run `r1k_ebx` added to `scripts/reproduce.sh`. Tests added. Documentation updated: method (*Cross spectrum with the dust template, and spectrum of the removed part*).
- **High-latitude spectra.** `scripts/make_figure_highlat.py` draws the ratio of the EE and BB spectra of u to the half-mission cross spectrum over |b| > 30 and 60 deg, for several runs (`--lmax 449`: above, the BB cross spectrum at |b| > 60 deg crosses zero); `scripts/reproduce.sh figures` makes it for `r256_fz_cl857`, `r256_joint_eebb` and `l256_fz_cl`. Paper: new figure in Sect. 5, and the joint (Q, U) network with the EE/BB spectrum loss (`r256_joint_eebb`) in the table, the text, the abstract and the discussion.
- **Spectrum terms `tcl` and `rcl`.**
  - `tcl`: the cross spectrum of the 857 GHz map with `u`, against that with `d`. The target is unbiased, since the noise is independent of the template. It gives TQ/TU, or TE/TB with `--pol-stats eb`.
  - `rcl`: the spectrum of `d - u` against that of the noise. On real data, the target is the noise of `d` itself, `C(d, d) - C(d1, d2)` (`NoiseSpectrumLoss.from_halves`). It is consistent with the target of `cl`, whereas the half-mission difference overestimates the noise of `d` by about 10 %. The runs `r1k_ref` and `r1k_res` (1000 steps) are added to `scripts/reproduce.sh` and `REPRODUCE.md`.
  - Both use the `--cl-*` options, with no noise draw and no bias (`stlia.spectra.NoiseSpectrumLoss`). `rcl` has its own masks and first multipole, `--rcl-masks` (default `30,45,60`) and `--rcl-lmin` (default 40): over the full sky and at large scales, its target is dominated by the Galactic plane.
  - With `--bias frozen`, the scattering term `res` now works too: Phi(d - u) against the noise statistics, the half-mission difference on real data. Before, it was ignored.
  - Tests added. Documentation updated: method (*Cross spectrum with the dust template, and spectrum of the removed part*).
- **Local spin-2 statistics.** `--joint --pol-stats chi` computes the data term on chi_E + i chi_B = conj-eth^2 (Q + iU) with the spin-2 engine of STL-Dev-MPI (`NativeEngine(spin=2)`, commit dab5bc6): a local operator, so masks with holes work, frame-independent, with live or frozen biases (`ChiTerms`). The Q and U noises of the same simulation are drawn together. With STL-Dev-MPI f4cf030, the cross terms are on chi too: `l1` and `l2` on pairs of spin-2 fields, `l3` on the scalar 857 GHz map against chi; the statistic masks work. Run `r256_chi` in `scripts/reproduce.sh` (engine built in `$R/stl_spin`). Also fixed: the `l256_fz_mk` line of `scripts/reproduce.sh` (run name glued to its first option). Documentation updated: method (*Local spin-2 statistics*), `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Statistics of the E and B maps.** `stlia-train-denoise --joint --pol-stats eb` computes every loss term on the E and B maps of the (Q, U) output (`stlia.polar.EBTransform`, spin-2 transforms of `stlia.sht`, differentiable), as the spin-2 statistics of FOSCAT on the full sky: they do not depend on the frame of Q and U. The observations and the noise simulations are transformed once; the noise biases are unchanged, the map being linear; with `cl` (spin 0), the spectrum term is on EE and BB. The spin-2 tables of `HEALPixSHT` now come from a Wigner-d recurrence in numpy (agreeing with exact values to 6e-14), no longer from `spherical`, which took minutes at nside 64; the ring FFTs of the transforms are batched by ring length instead of a Python loop over rings and modes. A test compares the maps with healpy. Documentation updated: method (*Statistics of the E and B maps*), user guide (full sky), API.
- **Noise-level conditioning.** `--cond sigma` (or `log857+sigma`) gives the network the log of the local noise level, the rms of the training noise simulations (Q and U variances averaged), standardised. Documentation updated: method (*Conditioning*).
- **Warm-up of the frozen biases, and warm start.** `--bias-warmup N` trains the first N steps (L-BFGS: rounds) with live biases before the first frozen estimate, so that it is made at a denoised u. `--init RUN/model.pt` starts from the weights of a previous run. Documentation updated: method (*Noise biases*), user guide.
- **Native U-Net run.** `scripts/train_native.slurm` trains the native U-Net with the driver of STL-Dev-MPI (b580210, same outputs as `train_denoise`). The run `r256_native`, as `r256_fz`, is added to `scripts/reproduce.sh` and `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Native U-Net benchmark.** `scripts/bench_native.slurm` times one frozen-bias step of the Rust U-Net of STL-Dev-MPI (`NativeUNet`, commit b580210) against the torch pipeline, at nside 64 and 256. Documentation updated: `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Joint (Q, U) network.** `UNet(maps=2)` and `stlia-train-denoise --joint` (real data, Adam): one network denoises Q and U together and every step trains both. The run `r256_joint_eebb` is added to `scripts/reproduce.sh` and `REPRODUCE.md`. `--cl-spin 2` puts the spectrum loss on the EE and BB cross spectra (`BinnedSpectra(spin=2)`), through the spin-2 `SpectrumEngine` of STL-Dev-MPI 92f2aef. The new tests cover the joint network and EE/BB against healpy. Documentation updated: method (*A joint (Q, U) network*), user guide (full sky).
- **Spectrum loss in the engine.** `BinnedSpectra(backend="stl")` and `stlia-train-denoise --cl-backend {auto,torch,stl}` compute the pseudo-spectra and their gradient with the `SpectrumEngine` of STL-Dev-MPI (commit 5648ba1). They agree with the `torch` transforms to 1e-12, and are 16 times faster on a T4 at nside 256. `auto`, the default, falls back to `torch` with an older engine. A test compares both backends. Documentation updated: method (*Spectrum loss*), installation, `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Runs `r256_cl857` and `l256_cl`.** The spectrum loss with live noise biases, for the network (with the 857 GHz conditioning) and for the L-BFGS reference. Its model has no noise bias in either scheme, since the half-mission noise draws are independent. Documentation updated: `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Masked statistics.** `--stat-masks` averages every scattering coefficient of the terms under galactic masks, each with its ergodic weight. It uses the masked averages of the STL-Dev-MPI engine (commit 9038114, `NativeEngine(masks=...)`), and `DenoiseLoss` gains the `masks` and `npix` arguments.
  - The runs `r256_fz_cl857_mk` and `l256_fz_mk` are added.
  - Documentation updated: method (*Masked statistics*), user guide, installation, `REPRODUCE.md`, paper Sect. 2.4.
- **Frozen noise biases.** `--bias frozen` implements the scheme of Delouis et al. (2022): the bias of each term is estimated at a fixed `u` with `--bias-realizations` draws and kept constant (re-estimated every L-BFGS round, or every `--bias-every` network steps), and the model is `Phi(u) + B`. The default, `--bias live`, keeps the previous behaviour.
- **Spectrum loss `cl`.** A binned half-mission cross spectrum under galactic masks (`--cl-masks`, `--cl-lmin`, `--cl-lmax`, `--cl-width`, `--cl-apod`). It comes with the modules `stlia.sht` and `stlia.spectra`: a differentiable ring-FFT spherical harmonic transform and `anafast`, adapted from healpix-analyse and FOSCAT and tested against healpy, and binned masked spectra. The optional extra `spin` covers the E/B transforms.
- **Conditioning maps.** `UNet(cond=...)` and `stlia-train-denoise --cond log857`: the network receives log I_857 at its input and at every decoder level.
- **Runs.** `l256_fz`, `r256_fz`, `r256_fz_cl857` and `l256_fz_cl` are added to `scripts/reproduce.sh` and `REPRODUCE.md`.
- **Documentation.** Updated: method (noise biases, spectrum loss, conditioning), user guide, installation, API. Paper: Sect. 2.4 and the conclusion of Sect. 5.
- **L-BFGS reference.** `stlia-train-denoise --optimizer lbfgs` uses no network: it minimises the training losses directly over the pixels of `u`, with the native L-BFGS of STL-Dev-MPI (`native_lbfgs`, strong-Wolfe line search). This is the per-map method of Delouis et al. (2022).
  - New options: `--lbfgs-rounds`, `--lbfgs-iter`, `--lbfgs-history`. The noise realisations are fixed within a round and redrawn between rounds.
  - The run `l256_dl123` is added to `scripts/reproduce.sh`.
  - Documentation updated: method (*The per-map reference*), user guide (*The L-BFGS reference*, outputs), `REPRODUCE.md`, and the paper (Sect. 2.3, "Per-map reference", and appendix).
- **Package.** The repository is now an installable package, `healpix-stl-ia`:
  - `pyproject.toml`, with commands `stlia-train-denoise` and `stlia-train-patches`;
  - the training programs move to `stlia.train_denoise` and `stlia.train_patches`, and `train_denoise.py` and `train_patches.py` stay as thin wrappers.
- **Documentation.** A Sphinx site, published on GitHub Pages:
  - installation, method, user guide, reproduction recipe;
  - command-line and API pages generated from the code.
- **Tests and CI.**
  - Unit tests: HEALPix geometry against healpy, the identity of the untrained network, gradients, absence of face edges, patches against the full sky, the loss, the command lines, and the engine gradient (when STL-Dev-MPI is installed).
  - Continuous integration: tests, documentation build with warnings as errors and deployment, and a check that every code change updates the documentation or this changelog.
- **Real-data mode.** `stlia-train-denoise --real` denoises the PR3 maps themselves: the full-mission and half-mission maps are the data. `scripts/make_figures_real.py` draws its figures.
- **GPU disc engine.** `stlia-train-patches` runs the disc statistics on the GPU with `--stl-backend gpu`, with the options `--cache-bytes` and `--reserve-bytes`.
- **Reproduction recipe.** `REPRODUCE.md` and machine-independent scripts: `scripts/env.sh`, `download_planck.sh`, `prepare_data.slurm`, `build_bridge.slurm`, `reproduce.sh`, `train_gpu.slurm`, `train_cpu.slurm`, `train_patches_gpu.slurm`.

### Changed
- **Paper.** Sect. 5 adds the frozen-bias L-BFGS reference `l256_fz` and a table comparing the network and the two L-BFGS references on the PR3 maps.
- **Paper.** Sect. 5 now gives the per-map L-BFGS reference `l256_dl123` (Fig. 11), with its maps and spectra compared to those of the network.
- `scripts/make_figures_real.py`: the full-sky figure shows the data `d` next to `u` and `d - u` (paper: figure caption updated).
- **Noise-bias wording.** The paper, the method page and the docstring of the L-BFGS reference now say that no noise bias is frozen: the statistics of `u + n_i` are recomputed at the current `u` at every evaluation. They said, wrongly, that the biases were re-estimated between batches of iterations as in Delouis et al. (2022).
- **Paper.** New section *Denoising the PR3 maps*: the network run `r256_dl123`, with the BICEP2 field, the EE/BB spectra against `d1 x d2` and the full-sky maps.
- The STL-Dev-MPI engine is imported when a training starts, so the package imports without it.
- `scripts/train_a100.slurm` is replaced by `scripts/train_gpu.slurm`, whose Slurm options come from the environment (`GPU_OPTS`).
